THE THROUGH LINE

Understand the why.
Engineer the how.

I'm Tousif—a software engineer who connects business needs, product experiences, and the systems that make them work.

At TriPoint Lending, I learned to turn stakeholder conversations into requirements developers could act on. At Clear One Advantage, that became a mobile product: one React Native codebase, two platforms, and 5,000 users. My Walmart internship brought full-stack delivery and observability into a retail environment reaching millions.

At JPMorgan Chase, the focus shifted to Python, PySpark, and AWS: more reliable production systems, faster pipelines, and a 40 TB data migration. Across those chapters, my approach has stayed consistent: clarify the problem, make the interfaces dependable, and measure what improves.

That curiosity also takes me into AI—from low-light computer vision at Drexel Vision Lab to agent-driven mortgage workflows, recommendation systems, and applied machine learning. I care about the whole path from an idea to something useful.
