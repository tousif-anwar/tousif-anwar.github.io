# Project video cards and mobile navigation

Walkthroughs live inside Projects cards. Decorative poster buttons are hidden when a matching video exists; their artwork remains available to the written case-study dialog. Each card has a click-to-load player, readable summary, close control, and external fallback link. Only one card player is mounted at a time. Filtering, opening a dialog, leaving the page, or hiding the browser tab stops card playback.

Scrimba's documented iframe format is the ordinary unlisted explainer URL without fullscreen or ownership parameters. Only exact Scrimba hostnames and /explain/guide... paths are embedded. No ownership claim token is published. Source: https://docs.scrimba.com/explain/embedding

Videos has been removed from the menu. The old videos.html URL redirects to projects.html so saved links continue to work.

Below 720px the header shows a three-line Menu button and utilities. Navigation remains collapsed until opened, supports aria-expanded/aria-controls, and closes on Escape, outside click, or navigation. Desktop navigation remains visible. Controls have at least 44px target height.
